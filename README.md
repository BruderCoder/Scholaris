# Scholaris 🎓

### Find the gap before it becomes the grade.

Scholaris is an **AI-powered education platform concept** designed to help students, teachers, and parents identify learning gaps before they turn into exam-time problems.

Instead of waiting for a student to perform poorly in a major examination, Scholaris uses **chapter-wise competency tests, learning-gap analysis, and recurring-mistake tracking** to identify areas that need attention early.

> **Note:** Scholaris is currently a **prototype/use-case demonstration**. The website showcases the intended workflow and user experience, but the underlying AI functionality and several backend features are not yet fully implemented.

---

## 💡 Why Scholaris?

A student may struggle with a single chapter without realizing it. If that gap goes unnoticed, the student can carry the misunderstanding into later topics and eventually make multiple mistakes in an examination.

Scholaris is designed to make these problems visible earlier.

It also addresses the other side of the classroom: teachers often spend significant time on repetitive administrative tasks instead of teaching and interacting with students.

Scholaris aims to bring these workflows together in one platform.

---

## ✨ Features

### 👨‍🏫 For Teachers

* **Quick Attendance** — Record attendance with just a few clicks.
* **Chapter Tracking** — Mark chapters as completed and initiate competency tests.
* **Learning Gaps** — Identify concepts where students are struggling.
* **Continuous Mistakes** — Detect recurring mistakes and possible misconceptions.
* **Student Insights** — Understand individual student performance more clearly.

### 👨‍🎓 For Students

* **Chapter-wise Assessments**
* **Progress Tracking**
* **Competency-based testing**
* **Upcoming assignments and tasks**
* **Personalized learning insights in the future**

### 👪 For Parents

The future version of Scholaris can allow parents to understand where their child is struggling and address those areas **before major examinations**.

### 🤖 AI

The proposed AI layer can:

* Analyze assessment performance
* Identify recurring mistakes
* Detect learning gaps
* Generate personalized competency tests
* Provide actionable insights to teachers
* Support students with basic academic queries

## 🔄 How it works

```text
Chapter Completed
       ↓
Competency Test
       ↓
Performance Data
       ↓
Learning Gap Detection
       ↓
Recurring Mistake Analysis
       ↓
Teacher / Parent Intervention
       ↓
Improved Understanding
```

The idea is to **identify the problem while there is still time to fix it**.

## 🛠️ How it was built

Scholaris was created using **AI-assisted development** to transform my product idea and workflows into a functional-looking web prototype.

The use of AI was primarily focused on development and implementation. The **problem statement, product ideology, feature concepts, and overall workflow were independently designed by me**.

The current implementation is primarily a visual and interactive demonstration of the intended product rather than a production-ready platform.

## 🎯 Vision

I want Scholaris to eventually become a **teacher intelligence layer** that connects:

**Attendance → Curriculum → Assessments → Mistakes → Learning Gaps → Student Growth**

The long-term goal is not to replace teachers with AI. It is to give teachers, students, and parents the information they need **early enough to make a difference**.
